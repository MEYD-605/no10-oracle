Historical performance dataset extracted from multi-year Google Ads campaign archives (`แว่นตาบอดสี` Search + `ดิสเพลย์เซล` Display) running from March 2017 to Q3 2023.

## 2. Demographic Profile (Audience Intelligence)

ConPass audience profile is the direct opposite of Club S Photography:
- **Gender**:
  - **Male: 67.52%** (497,714 impressions) — Matches medical reality (red-green color blindness is predominantly male X-linked).
  - **Female: 41.65%** (307,037 impressions) — Mostly mothers, partners, or HR finding solutions for family members.
- **Age Distribution**:
  - **25–34 years (36.08%)**: Working professionals needing color vision clearance for job applications, promotions, or offshore/medical checks.
  - **18–24 years (26.65%)**: Students/graduates applying for military, police, cadet, or driver's license exams.
  - **35–44 years (21.92%)**: Mid-career professionals and parents.
  - **45–54 years (10.53%)**: Secondary demographic.
- **Device Breakdown**:
  - **Mobile Phones: ~92%** (฿245,655.97 spend / 78,821 clicks)
  - **Desktop: ~6%** (฿16,831.08 spend / 5,952 clicks)
  - **Tablet: ~1%** (฿3,582.18 spend / 1,415 clicks)
