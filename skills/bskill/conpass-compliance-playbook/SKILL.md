## 2. Respectful Selection Guide (Do NOT Devalue Glasses)
- We sell both color blindness glasses and contact lenses.
- Do NOT trash or bash glasses (e.g. claiming they are bulky/useless).
- Present an objective **Selection Guide**:
  - **Glasses**: Best for everyday comfort, screen viewing, driving, and lifestyle without touching the eye.
  - **ConPass Contact Lenses**: Engineered specifically for discreet medical screenings (factory, transport, overseas work) where conspicuous tinted spectacle frames would draw examiner scrutiny.

## 5. Advertising & Regulatory Compliance
- **Safe Copywriting Rule**: Use commercial, discreet framing such as *"คอนแทคเลนส์เพื่อช่วยให้เห็นตัวเลข"* (contact lenses to help see numbers) and *"เตรียมความพร้อมก่อนตรวจสุขภาพ"*.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Avoid absolute exam pass guarantees (*"ผ่าน 100%"*, *"สถิติผ่าน 98.4%"*, *"โกงข้อสอบ"*).
- Target use cases safely: "แผ่นทดสอบ Ishihara Plate", "ตรวจสุขภาพเข้างานโรงงาน/ช่าง", "ตรวจโรคไปทำงานต่างประเทศ (EPS เกาหลี)", "สอบใบขับขี่".
- Avoid box abbreviation artifacts like "CP" in header logos; use clean brand logotypes.
- Header phone CTA can have attention micro-animation (`animate-call-halo`), but keep mobile sticky bottom bar actions clean and uncluttered.
