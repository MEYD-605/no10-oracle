# ConPass Ad Strategy, Copywriting & Clinical Landing Page Standards

Frameworks and operational guidelines for high-converting, professional-grade ConPass (คอนแทคเลนส์ตาบอดสี) advertising campaigns and landing page experiences.

---

## 1. Deep Pain Points & Psychological Objections
1. **Fear of Detection ("จะดูหลอกตา / คนสังเกตเห็นไหม?"):**
   - Customers worry about lens visibility during physical exams or transport tests.
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Emphasize natural appearance — "เลนส์สีเนียนกลืนกับม่านตา ไม่หลอกตา ดูเป็นธรรมชาติ".
2. **Usage & Acclimatization Confusion ("ใส่ข้างเดียวหรือสองข้าง? เคืองตาไหม?"):**
   - Customers need reassurance on usability and adjustment time.
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Highlight guidance — "มีคู่มือและเทคนิคการปรับสายตาก่อนวันตรวจจริง แนะนำการใช้งานเฉพาะบุคคล (สวมใส่ข้างที่มีความเด่นทางสายตา Dominant Eye)".
3. **Fear of Test Failure ("ซื้อไปแล้วจะอ่านออกจริงไหม?"):**
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Provide concrete visual proof via authentic Ishihara plate before/after demonstrations.

## 4. Professional Clinical Landing Page Standards (Opto-Lab CRO)

When designing or writing code for ConPass landing pages:

### A. Tone & Visual Identity
- **Clinical & Authoritative (Opto-Lab Standard):** Avoid consumer-grade e-commerce clutter or flashing discount popups. Use a structured, clean medical aesthetic.
- **Zero Casual Emojis:** Never use casual emojis (e.g. 🎯, 🚀, 💡, 🔥) in headers or body text. Use crisp SVG vector icons (Shield, Globe, Wrench, Eye Reticle, Truck, Checkmark) styled with cohesive tint palettes.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Dark Slate Header: `#0B1320`
  - Clinical Teal / Cyan: `#0D9488` / `#06B6D4`
  - Medical Trust Emerald: `#10B981` (used for CTA conversion buttons)
  - Background: Crisp White `#FFFFFF` and Slate Tint `#F8FAFC`
- **Authoritative Terminology:** Use correct optometric terms: *"Selective Spectral Filter"*, *"Ishihara Pseudoisochromatic Simulation"*, *"Optical Grade PolyHema Hydrogel"*, *"OPTO-LAB Device"*.

### B. Technical Optical Specifications (Mandatory Trust Component)
Always include a clear technical specification table on the landing page:
- **Material:** PolyHema Hydrogel (Optical Grade)
- **Water Content:** 38% (Low-dehydration formula for long-hour wear)
- **Base Curve (B.C.):** 8.6 mm (Standard Asian cornea fit)
- **Diameter (DIA):** 14.2 mm
- **Center Thickness:** 0.07 mm (@ -3.00D equivalent)
- **UV Protection:** UV-A & UV-B Cut Filter
- **Lifespan:** 1 Year (Annual Wear)

### C. Interactive Ishihara Simulation Requirement
Include an interactive simulation showing authentic dot patterns (e.g. Ishihara Plate 74) with a toggle button (*"ก่อนใส่ ConPass"* vs *"เมื่อสวม ConPass"*) so customers immediately grasp how the selective spectral filter separates overlapping wavelengths.

### D. Conversion Architecture & Mobile Optimization
- Over 90% of traffic is mobile: keep page assets lightweight, eliminate external heavy JS bundles, and use responsive viewport scaling.
- **Floating Sticky Bottom Bar:** Always present dual conversion channels:
  1. Phone Call button (`tel:...`) for urgent buyers.
  2. Full-width Emerald Green **"แอด LINE ปรึกษา / สั่งซื้อด่วน"** button linking directly to LINE OA (`https://lin.ee/...`).
- **Urgent Delivery Assurance Banner:** Explicitly state Grab Express 24h dispatch in Bangkok and Ladprao 48 pickup hub at the top and bottom of the page.
