# ConPass Ad Strategy, Copywriting & Clinical Landing Page Standards

Frameworks and operational guidelines for high-converting, professional-grade ConPass (คอนแทคเลนส์ตาบอดสี) advertising campaigns, domain setups, and landing page experiences.

## 1. Deep Pain Points & Psychological Objections
1. **Fear of Detection ("จะดูหลอกตา / คนสังเกตเห็นไหม?"):**
   - Customers worry about lens visibility during physical exams or transport tests.
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Emphasize natural appearance — "เลนส์สีเนียนกลืนกับม่านตา ไม่หลอกตา ดูเป็นธรรมชาติ".
2. **Usage & Acclimatization Confusion ("ใส่ข้างเดียวหรือสองข้าง? เคืองตาไหม?"):**
   - Customers need reassurance on usability and adjustment time.
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Highlight guidance — "มีคู่มือและเทคนิคการปรับสายตาก่อนวันตรวจจริง แนะนำการใช้งานเฉพาะบุคคล (สวมใส่ข้างที่มีความเด่นทางสายตา Dominant Eye)".
3. **Fear of Test Failure ("ซื้อไปแล้วจะอ่านออกจริงไหม?"):**
   - *Ad Copy / Page Angle:* Provide concrete visual proof via authentic Ishihara plate before/after demonstrations.

## 4. Professional Clinical Landing Page Standards (Opto-Lab CRO)

When designing or writing code for ConPass landing pages:

### A. Tone & Visual Identity
- **Clinical & Authoritative (Opto-Lab Standard):** Avoid consumer-grade e-commerce clutter or flashing discount popups. Use a structured, clean medical aesthetic.
- **Zero Casual Emojis:** Never use casual emojis (e.g. 🎯, 🚀, 💡, 🔥) in headers or body text. Use crisp SVG vector icons (Shield, Globe, Wrench, Eye Reticle, Truck, Checkmark) styled with cohesive tint palettes.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Dark Slate Header: `#0B1320`
  - Clinical Teal / Cyan: `#0D9488` / `#06B6D4`
  - Medical Trust Emerald: `#10B981` (used for CTA conversion buttons)
  - Background: Crisp White `#FFFFFF` and Slate Tint `#F8FAFC`
- **Authoritative Terminology:** Use correct optometric terms: *"Selective Spectral Filter"*, *"Ishihara Pseudoisochromatic Simulation"*, *"Optical Grade PolyHema Hydrogel"*, *"OPTO-LAB Device"*.

### B. Technical Optical Specifications (Mandatory Trust Component)
Always include a clear technical specification table on the landing page:
- **Material:** PolyHema Hydrogel (Optical Grade)
- **Water Content:** 38% (Low-dehydration formula for long-hour wear)
- **Base Curve (B.C.):** 8.6 mm (Standard Asian cornea fit)
- **Diameter (DIA):** 14.2 mm
- **Center Thickness:** 0.07 mm (@ -3.00D equivalent)
- **UV Protection:** UV-A & UV-B Cut Filter
- **Lifespan:** 1 Year (Annual Wear)

### C. Anti-Gimmick Rule: No Synthetic Dot Simulations & Mandatory Decision Matrix
- **Strict Prohibition:** Never build SVG, canvas, or hand-coded Ishihara simulation widgets. Programmatic dot patterns look artificial, cheap, and destroy clinical credibility.
- **Head-to-Head Decision Matrix:** Compare ConPass (฿1,490 / PolyHema Hydrogel / seamless Asian iris blend / Selective Notch Filter 570-590nm / 1-2h Grab dispatch) directly with **Other Contact Lens Brands in Market** (฿2,500-฿3,500+ / hard dry-eye plastics / unnatural red tint / uncalibrated red dye / slow pre-orders). Never compare against colorblind glasses.
- **Structured How-It-Works Protocol:** Guide buyers through a 4-step consultation, dominant eye assessment (Monocular Fusion), rapid delivery, and personalized Ishihara rehearsal video guide.

### D. Conversion Architecture & Mobile Optimization
- Over 90% of traffic is mobile: keep page assets lightweight, eliminate external heavy JS bundles, and use responsive viewport scaling.
- **Floating Sticky Bottom Bar:** Always present dual conversion channels:
  1. Phone Call button (`tel:...`) for urgent buyers.
  2. Full-width Emerald Green **"แอด LINE ปรึกษา / สั่งซื้อด่วน"** button linking directly to LINE OA (`https://lin.ee/...`).
- **Urgent Delivery Assurance Banner:** Explicitly state Grab Express 24h dispatch in Bangkok and Ladprao 48 pickup hub at the top and bottom of the page.

## 5. Domain Name Architecture & Technical Traps

### A. Thai IDN vs English Domain Decision Matrix
- **The Punycode Trap (Critical):** Thai IDN domains (e.g. `คอนแทคเลนส์ตาบอดสี.com`) automatically convert to Punycode (`xn--42ca2cdilcj0h5bd0ai2e6b0koa8r.com`) when copied into LINE, Facebook Messenger, SMS, or displayed in older webviews. This makes the URL look like phishing or malware, destroying ad conversion and customer trust.
- **Typo Risks:** Thai spelling variations (*คอนแทคเลนส์* vs *คอนเทคเลน* vs *คอนแทคเลน*) cause direct entry drop-offs.
- **Standard Protocol:**
  1. **Primary Domain:** Use a clean, authoritative English domain (e.g. `conpasslens.com` or `conpassoptics.com`).
  2. **Thai Keywords in URL Path:** Place Thai keyword slugs in the path (e.g. `conpasslens.com/คอนแทคเลนส์ตาบอดสี`) to gain ad Quality Score and SEO relevance without Punycode rendering issues.
  3. **Defensive Redirects:** If Thai IDN domains are purchased, set a 301 HTTP redirect forward to the primary English domain.

## 6. SEO & AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Standards

To ensure the landing page ranks in organic search and is cited by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google Gemini AI Overviews, Perplexity):

### A. Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data Graph
Always embed a complete `@graph` JSON-LD schema:
- **`Product` / `MedicalDevice`:** Detail brand (*ConPass*), price (*1,490 THB*), currency (*THB*), in-stock availability, and aggregate ratings (4.9/5).
- **`MedicalWebPage` & `MedicalCondition`:** Target *Red-Green Color Blindness (Deuteranomaly / Protanomaly)* to establish clinical topical authority.
- **`FAQPage`:** Embed 4+ structured Q&As answering high-volume questions (*"how does it work"*, *"monocular wear protocol"*, *"natural appearance"*, *"same-day Grab delivery"*).
- **`LocalBusiness`:** Specify the Bangkok emergency pickup hub (*Ladprao 48, Huai Khwang*) to capture local intent searches.

### B. In-Page Semantic Direct Answer Block (AI Knowledge Snippet)
- Include a dedicated semantic `<section>` containing a 40–60 word clear definition of the product, optical mechanism, and target use cases.
- Use concise bullet points for key specs so AI search crawlers can parse and cite the page as a featured snippet.
- Implement comprehensive **Open Graph (OG)** and **Twitter Card** meta tags with dedicated preview images for crisp link sharing across chat platforms.
